How to Play

  • Move: WASD / Arrow keys · Run: Shift · Interact: E · Pause: Esc
  • Follow the objective at the top. Touch glowing figures to talk (E).
  • Grace is your strength — hazards and enemies drain it. Rest at checkpoints (altars) to restore.
  • Reach the gate of light to complete each trial and learn its lesson.
  • Every trial teaches a real biblical truth. Rushing or choosing selfishly has consequences.
